DAILYKIT — BASIC PRIVACY NOTICE (prototype)

DailyKit is a browser-based utility prototype. Notes are saved in local browser storage on this device. Tool inputs are processed in your browser where possible. The QR generator loads a small JavaScript library from a public CDN; QR text is processed locally by that library and is not intentionally sent to DailyKit servers.

This prototype does not currently include advertising, analytics, accounts, cloud sync, or a backend.
